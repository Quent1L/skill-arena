Contesting a result doesn't take a rival score: the player says what they disagree with,
that reason opens the thread, and the reporter or an organizer corrects the entry.
Validation milestones are recorded there too, so a disputed match carries its own history.
Visible to its players and the organizers, live over WebSocket, and open as long as the
result can still be disputed.
