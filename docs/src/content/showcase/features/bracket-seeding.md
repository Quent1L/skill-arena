Brackets are generated in one action, in **single** or **double elimination** — the losers
bracket routing and the bronze match are handled for you.

Two seeding strategies are available:

- **Random** — a straight draw.
- **Championship-based** — seeds are derived from the standings of a finished championship.
  The source competition has to be finished and share the same discipline, and participants
  must actually have played finalized matches in it, so a final stage always reflects the
  season that earned it.
