Skol Arena supports exactly three tournament modes, each built for a different kind of event.

- **Bracket** — a single-elimination style event for a one-off tournament day: seed players or teams, play it out, done.
- **Championship** — an ongoing competition where standings persist across many matches over time, with configurable scoring and a full set of anti-abuse limits (rematch caps, per-player match caps, and more — see **Championship limits**).
- **Ranked** — a persistent competitive ladder with a real MMR rating system, dynamic rank tiers, and a live leaderboard. It's the most original mode in the app — see the dedicated **Ranked mode** spotlight below.

Bracket and Championship both support two team modes: **flex** (teammates are grouped per match, letting rosters shuffle round to round) and **static** (a fixed roster carries the same lineup across the whole event). Ranked's approach to teams is discipline-driven instead — see **Disciplines & outcomes**.
