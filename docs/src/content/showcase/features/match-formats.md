title: Match formats beyond 1v1
summary: Two sides, A and B — solo, fixed rosters, or teams assembled per match, with configurable team sizes.
order: 2

---

Every match opposes two sides, A and B, holding the same number of players each. Teams are
grouped per match (flex) or carried across the whole event (static), with a minimum and
maximum team size you set — so 1v1 chess, 2v2 foosball and 5v5 five-a-side all fit. N-way
formats such as 1v1v1 are on the roadmap.
