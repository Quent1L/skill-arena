---
title: Organizations
summary: Scope a tournament to a group — only its members can see it.
order: 11
icon: building
pillar: platform
docs: /docs/accounts#organizations
---

Attaching an organization hides a tournament from the listing _and_ closes it: standings,
matches and entries are all refused to non-members, so a shared link leaks nothing. A player
can belong to several organizations at once.
