---
title: Can players report their own matches?
order: 1
---

Yes. Any player taking part in a match can create it and report the result — no admin
needed. Depending on the tournament's validation mode, the result either locks in
immediately, waits for the opposing side to confirm, finalizes on a timer, or waits for an
admin. Players on the other side can confirm or contest. If they contest, the player who reported
the result can correct it, and the two sides can discuss it in the match thread — with the
tournament organizers notified in case they need to settle it. A player who contested can
also change their mind after that discussion and accept the result, which drops the
dispute and puts the match back in validation.
