---
title: Why Skol Arena
summary: Built for organizers who run tournaments regularly, not just once.
order: 0
---

Most tournament tools handle a single bracket and stop there — someone screenshots a spreadsheet, results live in a Discord thread, and the whole history resets for the next event. Skol Arena is built around the full lifecycle of a competitive community instead: recurring seasons, ranked progression, team management, and a history that survives across events.

It's aimed at the people running the same competitive group over and over — an office ladder, a campus league, an esports team's internal scrims, a board game club's monthly night — where "who's actually good" and "who won last time" matter beyond a single bracket.

Under the hood it's a Vue 3 frontend backed by a Hono/Bun API, with authentication, real-time updates, and a PostgreSQL database. It's self-hostable today, and open source under the AGPL-3.0 license.
