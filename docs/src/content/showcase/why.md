---
title: Why Skol Arena
summary: Built for organizers who run tournaments regularly, not just once.
order: 0
---

Most tournament tools handle a single bracket and stop there. Skol Arena is built
around the full lifecycle of a competitive community: recurring seasons, ranked
progression, team management, and a history that survives across events.

Under the hood it's a Vue 3 frontend backed by a Hono/Bun API, with authentication,
real-time updates, and a PostgreSQL database — open source and self-hostable.
