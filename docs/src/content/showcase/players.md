---
title: A competition your players run with you, not just play in
summary: Players report, confirm, dispute and cancel their own matches, and talk out the ones that go wrong — straight from their phone.
order: 0
---

Players aren't passive participants here. They're part of how the competition runs.

Instead of routing every result through one organizer, the players involved in a match
handle it themselves: report the score, confirm the opponent's report, contest one that
looks wrong, or cancel a match that shouldn't have happened.

Contesting doesn't need a counter-score. It flags the result as disputed, stops any
timer, and opens the discussion on the match itself, where the players and the organizers
work it out — then whoever reported the result corrects it.

Admin controls are still there when you need them: tournament admins and co-admins can
edit, finalize, or delete any match. The point is that the competition doesn't stall
waiting on them.
