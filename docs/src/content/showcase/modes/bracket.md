Use Bracket mode when you want the classic elimination experience. Single or double
elimination, losers bracket routing, and a bronze match are all handled for you.

It works standalone, but it also works as the closing stage of a longer competition:
seed the bracket straight from a finished championship's standings and the ranking your
players earned over the season decides who meets who.
