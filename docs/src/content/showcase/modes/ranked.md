Ranked stands on its own — it doesn't depend on a championship or a bracket. It's built
for ongoing competitive matches where players face each other whenever they want, climb a
real MMR ladder, and keep the competition alive between events.

Ratings run on an Elo-derivative tuned per season, rank tiers move with the player pool
instead of sitting in fixed bands, and a player's first ranked match enrolls them
automatically. Results must be reported within 48 hours of being played, so the ladder
reflects what actually happened.
