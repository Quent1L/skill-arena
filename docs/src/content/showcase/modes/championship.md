Championship mode is built for communities that want structure without the rigidity of a
full round-robin. Players don't have to face everyone, match volume is capped on purpose,
and the season can stretch across days or weeks to fit real availability.

Standings persist across every match, scoring is yours to configure (points per victory,
draw, and loss), and a set of fairness limits keeps a long season from being gamed: caps
on matches per player, on repeat pairings, and on repeat matchups.
